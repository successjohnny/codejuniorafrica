Build Your Personal Profile

Put your HTML and CSS knowledge into practice. Create a webpage that introduces you to the world!

Your mission

Create a profile page containing these elements:

  • Your name as a main heading.
  • A short introduction about yourself.
  • A list of at least three hobbies or interests.
  • Your favourite school subject or area of learning.
  • CSS styling with colours, spacing and borders.

Tip: Change the example code first, then experiment with your own ideas.
